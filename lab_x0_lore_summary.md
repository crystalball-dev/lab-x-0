# Lab X-0 (Zero Point) — Lore Summary & Design Guide

**LAB X-0 is a fan conceit.** No laboratory with that number exists in the *S.T.A.L.K.E.R.* games. The other LAB X-
plugins each borrow a real laboratory (X-3, X-10, X-19); X-0 imagines the one before all of them: Project X's
unnumbered origin site, the *нулевая точка* (zero point) from which every emission is counted. X-3 made
hallucinations, X-10 scorched minds, X-19 fused them. X-0 keeps time.

Everything the plugin plays is a real object of the Zone, read from the user's own copies of the games.

---

## 1. Canon the conceit is built from

* **Emissions (Выбросы).** Periodic catastrophic bursts from the centre of the Zone. Sirens and radio warnings
  give stalkers minutes to find shelter; whoever stays outside dies or is turned. Emissions reshape the anomaly
  fields and scatter new artifacts. They are the Zone's clock: X-0 is built around that clock.
* **Anomalies.** Localised traps with their own physics, named by stalkers: *Springboard* (Трамплин) throws,
  *Whirligig* (Карусель) lifts and spins, *Vortex* (Воронка) pulls in, *Burner* (Жарка) erupts in flame,
  *Electro* (Электра) discharges, the *Comet* moves along a path, the *spatial bubble* folds space.
* **Detectors.** Geiger counters tick with radiation; anomaly detectors beep faster as a trap gets closer; the
  advanced Veles detector draws anomalies and artifacts on a sweeping radar display.
* **Factions and voices.** Bandits ("cheeki breeki"), loners ("get out of here, stalker"), Duty, Freedom, the
  military, and the Monolith, whose fighters chant to the stone at the centre of the Zone.
* **Artifacts.** Objects formed in anomalies after emissions; found, carried, traded.
* **The X-labs.** Numbered underground laboratories of Project X (X-8, X-10, X-16, X-18, X-19 and others), where
  the Zone's psychic and physical experiments were run.

## 2. What This Means for the Sound

| Zone element | Sonic translation in LAB X-0 |
|---|---|
| The emission as the Zone's clock | the step sequencer locked to the host grid; EMISSION, the one-knob macro that pushes the whole punch chain |
| Ordnance and gunfire | kicks and snares from explosions, body falls and gunshots (ZONE KIT groups ORDNANCE and FIRE) |
| Detectors and weapon mechanics | hats and ticks from Geiger clicks, beeps, bolts and shell casings (CLICK) |
| Anomalies bending space | the TIME lane: Springboard repeats, Whirligig scratches, Vortex reverses, Comet tape stops, Jelly half speed, Burner accelerating ratchets, Bubble random stutter |
| Electro discharges | the VOLUME lane: gates, ducks, fades and mutes |
| Artifacts found after an emission | breaks: found loops to cut, re-order and re-pitch |
| Voices of the Zone | VOX: barks, chants and grunts as hits and break elements |
| The Veles detector | the EMISSION gauge: a radar sweep over the pattern |

## 3. Naming

Panels use the series' bilingual titles: `PATTERN // ВЫБРОС`, `PADS // АРСЕНАЛ`, `BREAKS // АРТЕФАКТЫ`,
`ANOMALY // АНОМАЛИЯ`, `PUNCH // УДАР`, `EMISSION // ВЫБРОС`, `LIBRARY // СХРОН`. Presets are named after places,
factions and objects of the Zone.
